# Kisaan AI
Kisaan AI is a mobile app that checks a tomato leaf for disease. You take a photo of one leaf, and the app tells you which disease it has and what to do about it.

It is made for tomato farmers in Pakistan. Many farmers can't reach an agriculture expert in time, so diseases are found late or treated with the wrong spray. This app gives them a quick first check on their own phone.

The app can detect 7 tomato leaf diseases (early blight, late blight, Septoria leaf spot, bacterial spot, leaf mold, yellow leaf curl virus and mosaic virus) and also tells if the leaf is healthy. It works offline, shows a heatmap of where the disease is on the leaf, and gives simple advice in Urdu.
